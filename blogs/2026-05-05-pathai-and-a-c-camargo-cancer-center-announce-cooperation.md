Boston, MA and São Paulo, Brazil – May 5th, 2026 – PathAI, a global leader in AI-powered digital pathology solutions, and A.C.Camargo Cancer Center, a national benchmark in oncological treatment, education, and research, are joining forces strategically to support the adoption of digital and AI-enabled pathology across Brazil. The collaboration reinforces the country's national efforts to modernize oncological diagnostics and strengthens PathAI's growing presence in the region.
