The microscopic examination of hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)-stained tissue has been the cornerstone of cancer diagnosis for over a century, relying on the trained eye of pathologists to detect cellular abnormalities that signal malignancy. In recent years, however, cancer care has grown increasingly complex. Especially with the advent and advancement of targeted therapeutics, there remains a need for biomarkers that would best match patients with available treatments.
