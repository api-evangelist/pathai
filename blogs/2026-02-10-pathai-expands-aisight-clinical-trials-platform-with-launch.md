BOSTON, MA — PathAI, a global leader in AI-powered pathology, today announced the strategic expansion of its support of inflammatory bowel disease clinical trials on the AISight® Clinical Trials Platform with the launch of IBDExplore to join AIM-HI UC. This launch marks a significant milestone for drug developers, making these advanced AI solutions immediately available for seamless integration into clinical trial protocols as standardized, reproducible endpoints.
